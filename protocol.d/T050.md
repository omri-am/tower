## Territories — `territories/<name>.md`

A territory is a set of paths that one sub-orchestrator plans for. Each territory has one
charter file, `territories/<name>.md`, where `<name>` matches `[a-z0-9][a-z0-9-]*`. The
directory is optional. Without it, the project has no territories and every card belongs to
`main`. In this version, `tower territories` declares and checks territories, and no command
starts a sub-orchestrator.

```yaml
---
name: docs               # equal to the file stem
role: orchestrator-docs  # the sub-orchestrator's mailbox role
approval: owner          # owner | delegated, with no default
---
```

The frontmatter has exactly these three fields. `role` follows the role rules of
[Durable role messages](#durable-role-messages): it is nonempty, it is not `.` or `..`, and
it contains no `/` or `\`. The suggested role is `orchestrator-<name>`, but any valid role is
accepted. `approval` has no default, so a charter without it is an error.

Body sections:

- `## Paths` — the territory's paths, one `- path` or ``- `path` `` bullet each. The syntax
  and the validation rules are those of a card's `## File ownership`. At least one path is
  required.
- `## Log` — dated `- ` lines that record changes to the charter, such as a grant of
  delegated approval.

The report path is not a field. It is always `territories/<name>/report.md`, so a charter
cannot point its report outside its own directory.

**Disjoint paths.** A path of one territory must not overlap a path of another territory.
The overlap test is the test that dispatch uses for card ownership.

**Card assignment.** The assignment ignores a card's own fragments: `changelog.d/<id>.md`,
`protocol.d/<id>.md` and `reference.d/<id>.md`. Every card owns one, and they never
conflict. A card belongs to a territory when each of its other paths is within a path of
that territory. A path is within a territory path when the two are equal. It is also within
when the territory path ends with `/` or holds a glob, and the literal prefix of the card
path starts with the literal prefix of the territory path at a directory boundary. Thus
`docs/agent` does not contain `docs/agent-messaging/x.md`. Every other card belongs to
`main`: it owns no other path, a path is within no territory, or its paths are within two
territories. A card whose ownership spans two territories belongs to `main`, never to one
of them.

**Approval.** The default is `approval: owner`. Under it, a sub-orchestrator writes and
refines draft cards in its territory, and only the owner moves a card from `draft` to
`ready`. Only the owner grants `approval: delegated`: the owner edits the charter or tells
the main orchestrator to edit it. The charter's `## Log` records the change with its date.
No orchestrator grants delegation to itself or to another orchestrator. Delegation lets the
territory's sub-orchestrator move its territory's cards from `draft` to `ready`, and nothing
more. The owner still merges every PR. Delegation never covers three kinds of change: a card
whose ownership spans two territories, a change to a charter's paths, and a change to a
charter's `approval` field. These stay with the owner.
