- `tower territories` now puts a card in `main` when one of its globs has a literal prefix
  that names a territory directory without the trailing `/`, such as `web*` for a `web/` or
  `web/**` territory. Such a glob also matches paths next to that directory, such as
  `web-api/`. Before, the card went to the territory. A glob whose literal prefix is inside
  the directory, such as `docs/**/*.md` or `docs/a*` for a `docs/` territory, and a plain
  path such as `docs` keep their results.
