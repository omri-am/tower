- `tower territories` no longer counts a card glob as within a territory path when the glob's
  literal prefix is the path's directory without the trailing `/`, such as `web*` for `web/` or
  `web/**`. Such a glob also matches paths next to that directory, such as `web-api/`. Before,
  the glob counted as within. A card that owns such a glob now goes to `main`, unless another
  territory path contains the glob. A glob whose literal prefix is inside the directory, such
  as `docs/**/*.md` or `docs/a*` for `docs/`, and a plain path such as `docs` keep their results.
