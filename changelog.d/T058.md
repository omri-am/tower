- `tower territories` assigns a card to `main` when one of its ownership globs has a fixed
  prefix that ends inside a path segment. For example, a card that owns `web*` no longer goes
  to a `web/` territory, because `web*` also matches `web-api/`. A glob whose prefix ends at
  `/`, such as `docs/**/*.md` in a `docs/` territory, and a plain path such as `docs` keep
  their results.
