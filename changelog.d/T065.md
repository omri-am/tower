- `tower-dispatch` in the default mode, with no `--headless`, `--here`, `--prep` or
  `--print-only`, now checks for `osascript` before it takes the dispatch lock. When `osascript`
  is not on `PATH`, as on Linux, it prints that the default mode needs `osascript`, names
  `--headless`, `--here` and `--prep`, and exits 1. It changes no lock, worktree, branch, card
  or commit. Before, it ran every other dispatch step first, such as the new worktree or the
  `--in-place` checkout and the card commit, and then failed at `osascript` with exit 127. A new
  dispatch then left its card `in-flight` with no session. Because the check comes before the
  lock, it also comes before the card status, dependency, ownership and `--expect-revision`
  checks.
- When `osascript` exits nonzero in the default mode, for example when macOS denies Terminal
  automation, `tower-dispatch` now prints
  `could not open a Terminal window (osascript exit <N>)` and says to use `--resume` after
  confirming that no session started. It still exits with the `osascript` status. Before, it
  printed nothing of its own.
