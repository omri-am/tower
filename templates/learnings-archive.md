# Retired learnings

> Out of every dispatch prompt, kept for provenance. Nothing is deleted from
> `learnings.md`; it moves here with the reason and the task that retired it.
> Format: `- [category] lesson — why it matters (T###) — retired T###: <reason>`.
> Reasons that count: superseded by a design change, proved wrong in a handoff, promoted
> into the skill or card template, or the code it described no longer exists.
